## About me

Hi! I am Jinwoo Jeong, a Ph.D. student in [Computer Systems Lab (CSL)](https://sites.google.com/korea.ac.kr/csl), Korea University. My advisor is [Prof. Jeongseob Ahn](https://jeongseob.github.io).

My research focuses on building efficient serving systems for deep neural networks and large language models (LLMs), especially memory management and serving scheduling in GPU-centric environments.

Feel free to reach out to discuss research, collaborations, or ideas!
