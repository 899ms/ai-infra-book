## **Jack Humphries**

jhumphri@cs.stanford.edu  
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jack-h-7b9a0a161/) / [GitHub](http://github.com/jackhumphries/) / [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/users/775624/jack-humphries)  
  
I am a Software Engineer at the Stellar Development Foundation. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Stanford University, focusing on operating system performance. I was advised by [Christos Kozyrakis](https://web.stanford.edu/~kozyraki/) and [David Mazières](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/~dm/).
