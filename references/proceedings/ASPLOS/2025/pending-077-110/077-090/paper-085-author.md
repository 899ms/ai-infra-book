# Daniel A. Jiménez

[TABLE]

I'm a Professor in the [Computer Science and Engineering Department](http://www.cse.tamu.edu) at [Texas A&M University](http://www.tamu.edu). My research is in microarchitecture and the interaction between the compiler and the microarchitecture. I've done a lot of work in branch prediction and cache management. I'm known for inventing the [perceptron branch predictor](http://people.engr.tamu.edu/djimenez/taco/pdfs/hpca7_dist.pdf) as well as for other research. I was elevated to **IEEE Fellow** in 2021 for my work in branch prediction. I recently won the [B. Ramakrishna Rau award](https://www.computer.org/press-room/2021-news/jimenez-to-receive-ieee-cs-rau-award) "for contributions to neural branch prediction in microprocessors."

Before I came to Texas A&M I was Professor and Department Chair in the CS department at UT San Antonio. Before that, I was an Associate Professor (with tenure) in the CS department at Rutgers. My Ph.D. in Computer Sciences is from UT Austin. I grew up in San Antonio, Texas. I'm a dual citizen of the USA and Mexico.

For a detailed list of professional things I've done, see [my CV](cv.pdf). Also see my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kajXr5oAAAAJ&hl=en) page.
