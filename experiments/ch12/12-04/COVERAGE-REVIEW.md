The final review distinguishes the original requirement from an added acoustic gate. The exercise explicitly allows calculation/network records; playback-records now supplies actual frame-consumption scheduling and stalls under a stated policy, beyond merely reporting first-ready bytes. This fulfills that form of first-play analysis. Speaker-to-microphone evidence is an unmeasured extension, not an explicit requirement. No volume change or microphone recording was performed. The earlier blanket acoustic gate was too strict; completing the calculation does not claim an audible measurement or a particular real player's behavior.

All required workload/factor records, source-result identities, contribution analysis and the permitted calculated playback boundary are now present. Historical12-4 is delivered with these explicit scientific limitations; this is not a claim of universal protocol performance or end-to-end acoustic validation.
