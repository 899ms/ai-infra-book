# Welcome to the PICTure Research Group!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

PICTure (**P**rogramming Systems and **I**ntelligent **C**omputing for Fu**ture**) is a research group at the [Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ECE) at the [Purdue University](https://purdue.edu), conducting research in the area of compiler technology and intelligent programming systems, with the emphasis on heterogeneous parallel computing, memory performance, data-intensive computing, high-performance machine learning, and real-time AI.

The group is led by [Dr. Xipeng Shen](xshen5/index.htm).
