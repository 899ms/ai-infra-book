# About Me

Hi, I am **Zhuoran Ji (纪卓然)**, an Assistant Professor in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at Shandong University.

I build systems that make ambitious computation practical—from operating systems for teams of coding agents to GPU-accelerated frameworks for privacy-preserving computing. My work sits at the intersection of operating systems, computer architecture, compilers, and security. I especially enjoy turning ideas that are powerful but painfully expensive into tools that people can actually use.

Before joining Shandong University, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong in 2022, advised by Prof. Cho-Li Wang. I also received my B.S. in Computer Science from HKU in 2018.

# Research Interests

- **Systems for AI agents:** operating systems, runtimes, file systems, and sandboxes for collaborative coding agents.
- **Privacy-computing systems:** GPU frameworks for fully homomorphic encryption and zero-knowledge proofs.
- **High-performance computing:** GPU algorithms, compilers, architecture-aware optimization, and multi-GPU systems.
