## Hello!

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science, University of Central Florida (UCF). I am leading the Compiler Architecture group at UCF. I generally develop architecture/compiler co-design solutions to improve performance, reduce hardware complexity, and address reliability/security problems. I received my Ph.D degree from [ Purdue University ](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/) under the guidance of [ Prof. Changhee Jung ](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/chjung) in 2022 and BS/MS from Kentucky State University. Prior to joining in UCF, I worked at [ ARM Research ](https://www.arm.com/resources/research) in 2020 with [ Prof. Fredrik Kjolstad ](http://fredrikbk.com/) in Stanford and [ COMMIT ](http://groups.csail.mit.edu/commit/) group in MIT. I also worked for NASA EpSCoR under [ Prof. Alexandre Martin ](https://www.engr.uky.edu/directory/martin-alexandre) at University of Kentucky (2017). I was a research engineer at [ LG Electronics ](https://www.lge.com/us) Car Laboratory (2012-2015). I am an IEEE Senior Member, and I was honored to receive an ACM SIGBED Paul Caspi Memorial Dissertation Award (2023).

I am actively looking for highly-motivated ~~postdoc~~/Ph.D students who are excited about computer architecture, compiler, and system research. Please email me your CV, if you are interested in the research.
