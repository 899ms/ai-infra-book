LPDDR5X memory
Low-power double data rate 5X (LPDDR5X) memory offers the best performance and power efficiency compared to LPDDR5, LPDDR4X and LPDDR4. It is designed for flagship smartphones, thin and light laptops and other mobile devices.

Frequently asked questions
What is LPDDR5X​ RAM​?
LPDDR5X, which evolved from LPDDR5, was designed to improve data transfer rates, power efficiency, bandwidth, density and overall performance for mobile devices.
​​​​​The LPDDR5X JEDEC standardization was published in July 2023 for a JEDEC compliant x16 one channel SDRAM device and x8 one channel SDRAM device.​
What are the benefits of LPDDR5X​ memory​?
LPDDR5X’s benefits are most noticeable in tasks that require rapid data access and processing while consuming relatively low power. These mobile applications benefit the most from LPDDR5X​ memory​:
High-end gaming: LPDDR5X​ memory​ allows for smoother gameplay, faster loading times and improved overall performance.
Camera and image processing: LPDDR5X​ RAM​ helps process and store images and videos quickly.
AI and machine learning: Applications that use on-the-edge AI and machine learning models for tasks like image recognition and language processing benefit from faster RAM for quicker data access during edge computation.
Augmented and virtual reality: AR and VR applications demand high-performance hardware like RAM. LPDDR5X helps provide a more immersive and responsive experience in AR and VR apps.
Other data-heavy applications such as multitasking, navigating and mapping, video editing and rendering are more likely to benefit substantially from LPDDR5X DRAM.
What is the difference between LPDDR5 and LPDDR5X mobile​ memory​?
LPDDR5X and LPDDR5 are both types of mobile DRAM used in smartphones and other mobile devices. But LPDDR5X offers improvements in terms of speed, power efficiency at higher performance levels, and lower latency.
LPDDR5 was more commonly found in smartphones, but LPDDR5X is expected to become more widespread in high-end flagship devices.
