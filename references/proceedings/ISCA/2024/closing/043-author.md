# 

My name is Yilong Zhao (赵怿龙), and I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My primary research interests lie in process-in-memory (PIM) architectures and AI accelerators. I am a member of the Advanced Computer Architecture Lab (ACA-IMPACT) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I am supervised by Professor [*Li Jiang (蒋力)*](http://www.cs.sjtu.edu.cn/en/PeopleDetail.aspx?id=165).

I received my Master's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2021 and my Bachelor's degree from the same university in 2018.
