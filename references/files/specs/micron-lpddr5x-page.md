### <span class="cmp-accordion__title" itemprop="name"> What are the benefits of LPDDR5X​ memory​? </span> <span class="cmp-accordion__icon"></span>

<div id="accordion-8447d9958e-item-baa6cd2cbe-panel" class="cmp-accordion__panel cmp-accordion__panel--hidden" cmp-hook-accordion="panel" role="region" aria-labelledby="accordion-8447d9958e-item-baa6cd2cbe-button" itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemscope="" itemtype="http://schema.org/Answer">

<div id="text-baa6cd2cbe" class="cmp-text" cmp-data-layer="{&quot;text-baa6cd2cbe&quot;:{&quot;@type&quot;:&quot;micron/components/content/text&quot;,&quot;repo:modifyDate&quot;:&quot;2026-08-21T16:12:58Z&quot;,&quot;xdm:text&quot;:&quot;&lt;p&gt;LPDDR5X’s benefits are most noticeable in tasks that require rapid data access and processing while consuming relatively low power. LPDDR5X helps provide a more immersive and responsive experience in AR and VR apps.&lt;/li&gt;\r\n&lt;/ol&gt;\r\n&lt;p&gt;Other data-heavy applications such as multitasking, navigating and mapping, video editing and rendering are more likely to benefit substantially from LPDDR5X DRAM.&lt;/p&gt;\r\n&quot;}}" ai-indexing="true" itemprop="text">

LPDDR5X’s benefits are most noticeable in tasks that require rapid data access and processing while consuming relatively low power. These mobile applications benefit the most from LPDDR5X​ memory​:

1.  High-end gaming: LPDDR5X​ memory​ allows for smoother gameplay, faster loading times and improved overall performance.
2.  Camera and image processing: LPDDR5X​ RAM​ helps process and store images and videos quickly.
3.  <a href="/markets-industries/ai" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">AI and machine learning</a>: Applications that use on-the-edge AI and machine learning models for tasks like image recognition and language processing benefit from faster RAM for quicker data access during edge computation.
4.  Augmented and virtual reality: <a href="/about/blog/company/innovations/ar-and-vr-the-next-generation-of-user-interface" target="_self" rel="noopener noreferrer">AR and VR</a> applications demand high-performance hardware like RAM. LPDDR5X helps provide a more immersive and responsive experience in AR and VR apps.

Other data-heavy applications such as multitasking, navigating and mapping, video editing and rendering are more likely to benefit substantially from LPDDR5X DRAM.
