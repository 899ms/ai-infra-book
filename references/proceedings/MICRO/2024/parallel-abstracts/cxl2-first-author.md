About Me

I am currently with the [Memory System Research Group](https://research.skhynix.com/research_areas/memory_systems_research?index=0) at [SK hynix America](https://www.skhynix.com). My research interests include computer architecture, memory systems, Compute Express Link (CXL), near-memory acceleration, heterogeneous computing, and systems for machine learning.

I received my Ph.D. in computer science from the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), advised by [Prof. Nam Sung Kim](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/members/). I was affiliated with the [FAST](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/) research group and Coordinated Science Lab.

Before joining UIUC, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering and Finance from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). I have worked as a visiting researcher at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/) and as a research intern at [Luminous Computing](https://luminous-co.squarespace.com) and [Intel Labs](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/overview.html).
