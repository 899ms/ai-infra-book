#### Bio

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National University of Singapore (NUS), advised by [Prof. Jialin Li.](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~lijl/). Before that, I received my Ph.D. from Peking University in June 2025, under the supervision of [Prof. Qun Huang](https://huangqundl.github.io/). I got my M.S. degree in Computer Architecture from Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT-CAS) in 2020, advised by [Prof. Qun Huang](https://huangqundl.github.io/) and [Prof. Yungang Bao](https://acs.ict.ac.cn/baoyg/). My research mainly focuses on network measurement, programmable network, and high-performance network.
