# About Me

I am an AI chip architect at ByteDance. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University (THU), where I was advised by associate Prof. [**Kaisheng Ma**](https://group.iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/~maks/leader.html) and worked closely with Prof. [**Dong Xiang**](https://www.thss.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/faculty/dongxiang.htm) and Prof. [**Torsten Hoefler**](https://htor.inf.ethz.ch/). I received my B.S. degree in Information Engineering with Zhiyuan honor degree and second bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at Shanghai Jiaotong University (SJTU).

## Work Scope (Research Interests)

- **AI Chip Architecture:** chip-level SPEC/Arch definition & tuning, preliminary architecture exploration, hardware/software co-design, performance modeling, and cross-functional alignment for PPA/RTL delivery
- **AI Infrastructure:** rack/cluster-level SPEC/Arch definition & tuning, AI scale-up/out network, communication library, distributed training/inference, performance/TCO optimization
