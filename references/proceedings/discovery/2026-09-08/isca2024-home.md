# ISCA 2024

![ISCA 2024 Logo](https://www.iscaconf.org/isca2024/images/ISCA_2024_v2.png)

### The 51^(th) International Symposium on Computer Architecture

## June 29–July 3, 2024

## Buenos Aires, Argentina

## The historic ISCA 2024 is now over!

  
It was such an honor to host you all in the beautiful Buenos Aires. For this **inaugural ISCA edition in Latin America**, we received 430 participants from more than 30 countries. The conference had a rich and diverse technical program including 83 technical papers over 19 sessions.  
  
ISCA 2024 also showcased the rich Argentinian culture, with a fantastic tango show and gala dinner at Tango Porteño, one of Argentina's most renowned tango venues.  
  
If you would like to share additional photos, feel free to reach out to [Augusto and Esteban](mailto:ajvega@us.ibm.com;emocskos@dc.uba.ar?subject=ISCA%202024%20Photos!) and/or post it in your social media profiles with tag \#ISCACONF2024  
  

### *See you all in [Tokyo](https://iscaconf.org/isca2025/)!*

### ISCA 2024 Award Recipients

July 10, 2024

**ACM SIGARCH – IEEE-CS TCCA Outstanding Dissertation Award**

- Tanvir Ahmed Khan, “Rescuing Data Center Processors”
- Poulami Das, “Software and Architecture Techniques for Improving Fidelity of Emerging Quantum Computers” (Honorable Mention)

**2024 TCCA Young Architect Award**

- Mengjia Yan, “In recognition of outstanding contributions to secure architectures and formal verification of architectural defenses”
- Prashant Nair, “In recognition of outstanding contributions to mitigate scaling-related DRAM faults and security vulnerabilities”

**2024 Maurice Wilkes Award**

- Reetuparna Das, “For contributions to in-memory computing”

**2024 ISCA Influential Paper Award**

- “Exploiting ILP, TLP, and DLP with the polymorphous TRIPS architecture” by Karthikeyan Sankaralingam, Ramadass Nagarajan, Haiming Liu, Changkyu Kim, Jaehyuk Huh, Doug Burger, Stephen W. Keckler, and Charles R. Moore

**2024 ISCA Best Paper Award**

- Constable: Improving Performance and Power Efficiency by Safely Eliminating Load Execution by R. Bera, A. Ranganathan, J. Rakshit, S. Mahto, A. Nori, J. Gaur, A. Olgun, K. Kanellopoulos, M. Sadrosadati, S. Subramoney, O. Mutlu

**2024 ISCA Distinguished Artifact Award**

- Tetris: A Compilation Framework for VQA Applications in Quantum Computing by Y. Jin, Z. Li, F. Hua, T. Hao, H. Zhou, Y. Huang, Z. Zhang
- FireAxe: Partitioned FPGA-Accelerated Simulation of Large-Scale RTL Designs by J. Whangbo, E. Lim, C. Zhang, K. Anderson, A. Gonzalez, R. Gupta, N. Krishnakumar, S. Karandikar, B. Nikolic, S. Shao, K. Asanovic
- DaCapo: Accelerating Continuous Learning in Autonomous Systems for Video Analytics by Y. Kim, C. Oh, J. Hwang, W. Kim, S. Oh, Y. Lee, H. Sharma, A. Yazdanbakhsh, J. Park
- The Dataflow Abstract Machine Simulator Framework by N. Zhang, R. Lacouture, G. Sohn, P. Mure, Q. Zhang, F. Kjolstad, K. Olukotun

**2024 SIGARCH Alan D. Berenbaum Distinguished Service Award**

- Timothy Pinkston, “For decades of exemplary research service spanning computer architecture and the wider research community via his leadership at NSF, ACM, and broadening participation”

**2024 ACM/IEEE-CS Eckert-Mauchly Award**

- Wen-mei Hwu, “For seminal contributions to architectures, compilers, and adoption of multiple generations of processors, including superscalar, VLIW, and GPUs”
