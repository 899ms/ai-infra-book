Same actual WAV and source availability trace as tts-network. Compare recorded294 source-read segments,64KiB PCM coalescing (WAV header separate), and whole-response buffering. A coalesced group cannot be released before the last source byte it contains is available; no synthetic bandwidth or faster model is assigned. Whole-response buffering includes the WAV header. Fixed CPU/memory,image,protocol versions,source bytes and network profile.

The kernel's random-drop samples can differ; preserve seeds/counters and do not claim exact same packet losses. Three outer trials shuffle the three chunk modes with seed120404. Each subrun has one warmup per protocol, then two sequential requests per protocol×reuse condition, shuffled by trial. Total9 subruns,90 requests (72 formal,18 warmups). Mode ordering is recorded before execution. All modes are rerun in this batch; previous timing results are not substituted for the control.
